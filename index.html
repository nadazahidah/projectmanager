05 / HOW I WORK

I care about how the work gets there.

A campaign can have a great idea and still become messy in execution. I tend to pay attention to the gaps between people, information, and deadlines.

01

I understand before I distribute.

Before turning a brief into creator communication, I need to know what actually matters: the objective, message, deliverables, timeline, and non-negotiables.

02

I don't believe every creator needs the same approach.

A brief needs enough structure to protect the campaign, but enough room for the creator to still sound like themselves.

03

I keep an eye on the gaps.

Missing information, unanswered chats, pending revisions, unclear expectations — small things can become big delays if nobody catches them early.

04

I translate between people.

Sometimes the problem isn't the content. It's that the brand, creator, and internal team are imagining three different things. That's where alignment matters.

05

I like closing loops.

Done isn't “the creator posted.” Done means the deliverable is accounted for, the proof is there, and nobody is left wondering what happens next.
